# Callout

An aside inside an article for a tip, note or warning that the reader shouldn't miss.

Default is `accent-soft` with an accent icon; `q-callout--warning` tints with `warning`. Text is ink at 16/24, starting with a short bold lead-in ("Note.", "Heads up."). Use sparingly — more than one or two per post and they stop working. No left-border stripe. Consumer provides the content and variant.
