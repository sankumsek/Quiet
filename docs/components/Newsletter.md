# Newsletter

A subscribe block for the end of posts or the home page: heading, one honest sentence, email field and a primary button.

Reuses TextField (`q-input`) and Button (`q-btn--primary`) — this is the one primary button on a post page. Stacks vertically on phones. Say plainly what people get and how often. After submit, replace the form with a one-line confirmation ("Check your inbox to confirm."). Consumer provides the form action.
