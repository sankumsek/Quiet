# PostCard

A post teaser for home pages and topic pages, laid out in a responsive `q-cards` grid.

The whole card is one link: cover image (16:9, `surface-sunken` while loading), one tag, title in 20/25 semibold, a one-line excerpt in `ink-secondary`, and date · reading time in `ink-tertiary`. `surface` with `radius-lg` and `shadow-1`, lifting to `shadow-2` on hover (no lift with reduced motion). Covers are optional — without one the card still reads. Consumer provides cover, tag, title, excerpt, date and link.
