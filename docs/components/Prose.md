# Prose

The long-form reading styles: wrap a post's rendered HTML (Markdown output works as-is) in `.q-prose` inside `.q-measure`.

Body is `article-body` (19/30, 18/28 on phones) with a `space-6` paragraph rhythm. `h2` and `h3` use `article-h2` / `article-h3` with more space above than below, so headings belong to what follows. Links are `accent` with a soft underline that firms up on hover. Lists use `ink-tertiary` markers. Blockquotes are larger text with an accent quote mark — no left border. Inline `code` sits on `surface-sunken`. `hr` renders as three quiet dots. Don't nest cards or buttons inside prose; use Callout, Figure and CodeBlock for anything that isn't text.
