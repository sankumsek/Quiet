Notes on building quietly

What I learned designing a small system for my own projects, and why fewer choices made it better.

SSanjeevOct 15, 20266 min read

Prose

Guidelines

The long-form reading styles: wrap a post's rendered HTML (Markdown output works as-is) in `.q-prose` inside `.q-measure`.

Most design systems start big and get pruned. This one went the other way: a handful of tokens, a few components, and a rule that anything new has to earn its place.

Why fewer tokens

Every token is a decision someone has to make later. With surface, surface-sunken and surface-raised, there are only three answers to “what goes behind this?”

  • One accent for anything you can act on.
  • System colours for identity, never for buttons.
  • Materials only for things that float.
Simplicity is not the absence of clutter; it's the presence of clear decisions.— A note to myself

A smaller point

Line length matters more than font choice. Keep the column near 680px and most faces read well.
